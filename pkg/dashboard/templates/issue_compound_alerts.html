Compound issue alerts

Require event frequency and verified distinct customer impact in the same rolling window. Reported customer hints do not count. Only a new accepted event inside the window can trigger an alert; no alert is replayed when a rule changes or a cooldown expires.

Native Slack delivery uses the issue's assigned team or the configured fallback team. Enable compound thresholds in Slack settings, then choose channel rules by alert type and rule key. Webhooks can subscribe to issue.compound_threshold_reached.
