Capture now

Profile one running instance for a short window without redeploying. CPU shows where time goes; heap shows memory that is still held (Node and Python: allocated during the window and still live at its end); stacks is an instant snapshot of where every goroutine or thread is waiting, for requests that hang. Block and mutex (Go only) show where goroutines waited on channels and locks, and on contended mutexes, during the window.

Profile kinds

The app must be running: captures do not wake a parked app. One capture per app runs at a time; captures are kept for 7 days. When an instance runs low on memory, Gregale captures its heap automatically (at most once per hour per app); when a request stays in flight for 10 seconds, it snapshots the instance's stacks (at most once per 15 minutes per app).
