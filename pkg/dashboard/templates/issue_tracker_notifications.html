Notification settings

Notifications are opt-in. Pauses and held provider closures alert once per incident kind. Repeated errors alert at the configured threshold; a confirmed successful automatic check sends recovery. Inspections and re-enabling alone never announce recovery. Settings changes start fresh incident tracking at the next automatic check; existing paused policies are not replayed.

Optional app webhook subscribers can receive issue.tracker_sync.attention and issue.tracker_sync.recovered. Webhooks carry routing metadata; this does not register email or chat destinations.

Unacknowledged incident reminders

Reminders are opt-in and require notifications to be enabled. Up to {{.RemindersMax}} reminders per incident route to its current eligible team or account, with owner fallback. Acknowledgment or confirmed recovery stops new reminders. Snoozes pause reminders; after expiry, a full interval passes before another reminder. Claims alone do not acknowledge an incident.

Enabling or changing the interval starts a full waiting interval. Reconfiguration does not reset an incident’s reminder budget. Reminders appear in this inbox and use issue.tracker_sync.attention with kind reminder for subscribed app webhooks. Already queued deliveries follow existing webhook controls.
