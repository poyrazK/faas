Tracker sync notifications

Notifications route to the assigned team or account at the time of the alert, with app-owner fallback when no eligible recipient exists. Membership alone does not grant app access. Captured recipients may read their notifications and linked local health; general issue access and provider controls remain with the app owner. Removed members cannot read team notifications here.

Notification settings

Notifications are opt-in. Pauses and held provider closures alert once per incident kind. Repeated errors alert at the configured threshold; a confirmed successful automatic check sends recovery. Inspections and re-enabling alone never announce recovery. Settings changes start fresh incident tracking at the next automatic check; existing paused policies are not replayed.

Optional app webhook subscribers can receive issue.tracker_sync.attention and issue.tracker_sync.recovered. Webhooks carry routing metadata; this does not register email or chat destinations.
