# Sales workspace and referral attribution

Referral URLs and first-touch tracking are superseded by [short referral codes](20261103190000-short-referral-codes.md).
- **Status:** accepted; deployment remains operator controlled
- **Date:** 2026-11-03
- **Decision:** serve Gregale's sales workspace from apid at `sales.<platform-domain>`, with a customer referral page on the existing dashboard. Use the existing live sessions, email verification, MFA, CSRF origin checks, PostgreSQL and single public edge. Sales membership and manager membership are explicit startup allowlists, independent of operator permissions.
- **Context:** the product console manages a user's workloads and downstream customers. Gregale's own sales team needs acquisition information, follow-ups and referral links without infrastructure administration. Customers also need personal referral links and private reward status.
- **Attribution:** high-entropy links distinguish sales and customer sources. An HMAC-authenticated, host-only, Secure/HttpOnly cookie records the first valid visit for thirty days. Links land on the API host and continue through the existing signup flow, including OAuth. The canonical new-account transaction records at most one attribution and snapshots program terms. Existing accounts cannot claim or replace attribution. Paused links, inactive owners, and removed sales members cannot attribute new signups.
- **Conversion:** the invoice snapshot writer atomically retains the first observed paid invoice and observation time. Duplicate notifications preserve both. The conversion window starts at signup, and the reward hold starts at observed payment, including invoices created as drafts earlier. Refunds, pending refunds, voids, missing evidence, unverified customers, inactive parties and currency mismatches prevent eligibility. A later payment never replaces refunded evidence. Commission is a one-time basis-point share of the first invoice's pre-tax paid amount, rounded down to whole cents.
- **Rewards:** customer and referred-customer fixed rewards, sales commission, currency, hold and conversion windows are configurable. Defaults grant no monetary rewards. Existing attribution terms are immutable when settings change. Values are estimates: no cash payment, credit, commission disbursement or payment-provider mutation is implemented or enabled.
- **Access:** ordinary customers see only their outgoing referral status and reward estimates, with contact details, account identifiers, notes and payment amounts removed. Reps see their own referrals; managers see the team and customer referral program. Sales reads require a live, MFA-completed session of an enrolled, verified team member; API keys are refused. Managers do not gain operator privileges.
- **Consequences:** sales and customer acquisition metadata live outside environment clone scope. Source and referred-account deletion cascade acquisition records. Follow-up notes are bounded, private to the authorized staff view, and excluded from audit payloads. Data and assets return `no-store`. The feature is disabled by default and all feature routes return 404 until enabled. The `sales` app label is reserved under platform routing.
- **Alternatives:** a second credential system or a standalone CRM would duplicate identities and customer-data authority. Automatic payouts would require separate financial, fraud, refund, accounting and provider qualification; that is deliberately outside this implementation.
