# ADR-20261023215834 · Event plan caps: settled retention, retained budgets and parked workflow runs

- **Status:** proposed. The values below are recommendations for the owner;
  nothing changes until this is accepted.
- **Date:** 2026-10-23
- **Builds on:** ADR-615 (customer event storage admission), ADR-614 (event
  delivery backpressure), ADR-649 (fair bounded workflow dispatch), ADR-1430
  (plan entitlements from the October 2026 COGS report),
  ADR-20261022105723 (queue backlog caps), spec Appendix D V12.
- **Context:**
  - **Measured capacity.** The provisional V12 run
    (`docs/ops/evidence/20261022-v12-provisional`) sustains 20 events/s end
    to end in every run on the 2 vCPU test node, and about 50/s on a quiet
    host. #5182, #5226 and #5230 since cut the event path's database work.
    V12 places caps at no more than half the sustained rate, so until the
    reference hardware is measured, no single account should be able to
    sustain more than 10 events/s.
  - **Publication caps today.** ADR-615 bounds retained customer events per
    account, and a settled receipt stays charged for 30 days. The budget is
    the sustained rate:

    | Plan | Retained events | Retained bytes | Sustained over 30 days |
    |---|---:|---:|---:|
    | Free | 4,096 | 8 MiB | 137 a day |
    | Hobby | 16,384 | 64 MiB | 546 a day |
    | Pro | 131,072 | 512 MiB | 4,369 a day (0.05/s) |
    | Scale | 1,048,576 | 4 GiB | 34,953 a day (0.4/s) |

    A Scale account publishing one event a second fills its budget in
    twelve days and is refused for the rest of the window.
  - **What settled receipts are kept for.**
    - Duplicate acceptance: the same source and id within the window returns
      the original receipt.
    - Event recovery jobs.
    - Replay preview and backfill (`EventReplayBackfillMaxRange`, 30 days).
    - The execution ledger (`EventExecutionLedgerRetention`, 30 days).

    All of them read the outbox row. Pruning (`PruneDeliveredPublishedEvents`)
    uses the fixed `PublishedEventIdentityRetention`, skips receipts that an
    active backfill job holds, and never prunes a pending receipt.
  - **What a receipt costs.** ADR-615 charges the UTF-8 envelope, the
    separate `event_data` copy and the immutable recipient snapshot: about
    twice the event's data, plus the envelope fields and a few hundred bytes
    per matched recipient. The Scale budget allows 4 KiB per event.
  - **Workflows.** A run waiting on an event (`awaiting_event`, for up to
    30, 90 or 365 days by plan) counts against `WorkflowMaxConcurrent`
    (10 / 50 / 200 per app). A Pro app with 50 runs parked on events can start no new
    run. Dispatch runs 4 slots per node, 2 per app (ADR-649).
  - **Queues** were re-sized by ADR-20261022105723 and are out of scope.
- **Decision (recommended):**
  1. **Settled retention per plan.** A settled receipt is kept, identity and
     payload together, for 1 / 3 / 7 / 14 days on Free / Hobby / Pro /
     Scale instead of 30 days for every plan. Duplicate acceptance, event
     recovery and replay ranges follow the same window. Pending receipts are
     never pruned, as today.
  2. **Retained budgets sized to a sustained rate.** Each budget covers the
     plan's window at a target sustained rate, rounded down to a power of
     two, with 2 KiB per event:

     | Plan | Window | Retained events | Retained bytes | Sustained rate |
     |---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
     | Free | 1 day | 4,096 (unchanged) | 8 MiB (unchanged) | 0.05/s, 4,096 a day |
     | Hobby | 3 days | 131,072 | 256 MiB | 0.5/s, 43,690 a day |
     | Pro | 7 days | 1,048,576 | 2 GiB | 1.7/s, 149,796 a day |
     | Scale | 14 days | 8,388,608 | 16 GiB | 6.9/s, 599,186 a day |

     The largest per-account rate stays under the provisional V12 bound of
     10 events/s.
  3. **Parked workflow runs leave the concurrency cap.** A run in
     `awaiting_event` counts against a new per-app `WorkflowMaxParked` of 100 /
     1,000 / 10,000 on Hobby / Pro / Scale instead of `WorkflowMaxConcurrent`.
     A run that resumes takes a concurrent slot again and waits for one if
     none is free. Dispatch slots stay as they are until a workflow benchmark
     measures them.
- **Consequences:**
  - Customer-visible: duplicate acceptance, recovery and replay windows
    shrink from 30 days to the plan window. Docs, the OpenAPI sources and
    `GET /v1/events/storage` report the window.
  - Receipts older than the new window are pruned by the existing sweep in
    its bounded batches after release; backfill jobs keep their pins.
  - Storage at full budget rises 4× per paying account (Scale 4 → 16 GiB).
    Control-plane disk sizing must cover the expected number of accounts at
    their budgets.
  - Downgrades and over-budget accounts keep ADR-615's behaviour.
- **Alternatives considered:**
  - *Raise the budgets, keep 30 days.* Scale at 7 events/s retains 18M
    events, 36 GiB at 2 KiB each, per account, and every plan's storage
    grows with it. Dedupe and replay windows would not change.
  - *Keep identities 30 days, drop payloads after the window.* Smaller
    product impact on dedupe, but every outbox reader (recovery, replay,
    execution ledger) would have to handle a compacted receipt. A possible
    follow-up if the shorter dedupe window proves too short.
  - *A per-account publish rate limit on today's budgets.* It cannot raise
    the sustained rate the 30-day charge allows.
- **Implementation sketch:**
  - `pkg/api/limits.go`: an `EventSettledRetention` per plan, the new
    `EventStorage` values and `WorkflowMaxParked`.
  - Pruning computes each account's cutoff from its plan; replay range,
    recovery and duplicate checks use the same window.
  - The workflow admission count splits `awaiting_event` from
    `pending`/`running`; resume admission takes a concurrent slot.
  - No schema change is expected for 1 and 2. Tests: pruning per plan at the
    window boundary, duplicates inside and outside the window, backfill pins,
    and parked versus running admission.
- **Open questions for the owner:**
  1. Are the shorter duplicate and replay windows acceptable per plan?
  2. Financial model: control-plane storage cost per GiB-month on the OVH
     launch hardware, and how many accounts at full budget the disk must
     hold.
  3. The rates are provisional until V12 runs on the reference hardware.
     Should they wait for that run, or ship now and be revisited?
