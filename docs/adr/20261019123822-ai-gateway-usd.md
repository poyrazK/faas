# ADR 20261019123822 · AI gateway prices, limits and costs in US dollars

- **Status:** accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Amends:** ADR-1430 (Gregale bills in US dollars; the AI gateway price catalog kept its own currency)
- **Relates to:** ADR-990 (prices, cost limits), ADR-994 (price catalog), ADR-1436 (tag cost limits)
- **Decision:**
  - **Every AI gateway amount is in the billing currency, US dollars.** This covers price lists, the built-in catalog, daily and monthly cost limits, end-user and tag cost limits, settled costs, savings and budget alerts. The amounts stay integer millicents: 100,000 millicents are one dollar.
  - **The catalog takes LiteLLM's US-dollar list prices as they are,** rounded up to whole millicents, with no exchange rate. Its header names the currency (`"currency": "USD"`) in place of `usd_per_eur` and `fx_source`; a catalog in any other currency does not parse.
  - **The catalog response gains `currency`.** It keeps `usd_per_eur` (always `"1"`) and `fx_source`, deprecated, so released SDKs that require them still parse it. A later release removes them.
  - **Stored amounts are read as dollars without conversion.** Price lists and cost limits written in euros before this change take the same numbers in dollars. The gate has been off in production and no customer has an AI integration, so nothing is re-priced in practice; a deployment that has euro amounts should re-enter them.
  - **The CLI enters and shows dollars** (`input=0.15` is $0.15 per million tokens). Dashboards show costs in USD.
- **Why:**
  - ADR-1430 moved every charge to US dollars but left the gateway in euros. The same customer then set an AI cost cap in euros and paid their bill in dollars. The CLI's money formatter already printed the billing currency, so it labelled euro AI amounts "USD".
  - The providers price in US dollars. A euro catalog carried an exchange rate fixed at generation time, which drifted from the providers' prices with every rate move.
- **Consequences:**
  - **Catalog numbers rise by the old exchange rate:** a model listed at $1.25 per million tokens now reads 125,000 millicents instead of 111,548.
  - **Caps no longer move with the exchange rate;** they move only with provider prices.
  - **Only the catalog header changes in the file format.** Its rows keep their 16 fields.
- **Rejected alternatives:**
  - **Converting stored euro amounts at a rate.** No production data exists to convert, and a conversion would have to choose a rate and a date.
  - **A currency per integration.** Customers pay Gregale in one currency and their providers mostly in dollars; a second currency only moves the confusion.
  - **Removing `usd_per_eur` and `fx_source` now.** Released Python and Node SDKs require them in the response.
