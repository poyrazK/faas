# ADR: Sales lead pipeline and follow-up inbox

Status: Accepted 2026-11-03

## Context

The sales workspace shows referred customer accounts. Salespeople also need to
work with prospects before signup, keep ownership clear, and track next actions.
The first release adds leads, ownership and in-app task reminders. Team access
continues to use the existing verified-email, MFA and explicit sales allowlists.

## Decision

Store prospects in `sales_leads` and follow-ups in `sales_tasks`. Apid owns both.
A lead has a name, normalized email, optional company and notes, a salesperson,
and a manually selected new/contacted/qualified/demo/won/lost stage. Email is
unique per owner, allowing separate reps to record their own introductions.
Tasks have a title, a calendar due date, completion state and the lead reference.
Deleting a lead deletes its tasks. All records remain outside app clones.

Reps see and edit their own leads and tasks; managers have team-wide access.
Only managers may assign or reassign another salesperson. Assignment requires
an active, email-verified, MFA-enrolled account in the configured sales team.
Task permissions derive from the lead on every read and write, so reassignment
moves the tasks atomically with the lead. Optimistic versions reject stale edits
and deletes. A manager can recover leads left behind by a revoked team member.

Derive referral matching at read time from an exact normalized email and an
actual sales referral credited to the current lead owner. Show signup/payment
signals only when both match. Other reps' referrals, customer-program referrals
and un-attributed existing accounts cannot reveal account existence through CRM
lookups. Reassignment may remove a match; it never transfers historical credit.
Stages, tasks and CRM deletion never change invoices, attribution or payouts.

Lists use bounded pagination with encrypted cursors bound to actor, manager
scope, resource and filters. Leads use descending UUIDv7 order (newest first); tasks use due date plus UUID.
Search runs across all permitted leads. Task totals cover the selected owner
scope and exclude completed tasks. The browser sends its local calendar day for
consistent today/overdue filters; non-browser clients may supply `on=YYYY-MM-DD`,
otherwise the API uses UTC. Task dates are calendar dates, not scheduled sends.

## Consequences

The existing feature flag and staff portal protect the new private routes.
Apply the additive migration before deploying apid. No new daemon, external CRM,
outbound message, payment integration or customer-console release is required.
Reminders appear in the task inbox and navigation while the portal is open;
visible pages refresh tasks every minute. This release does not send email,
calendar invitations, browser notifications or messages to prospects.
