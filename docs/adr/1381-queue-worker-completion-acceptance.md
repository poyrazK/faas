# ADR-1381: Queue worker completion acceptance

- **Date:** 2026-10-15
- **Status:** accepted; native execution pending
- **Context:** Queue completion-canary APIs, durable verification receipts and release fences exist, but the shipped `queue-worker` treats canary payloads as ordinary jobs. Existing parallel-delivery metal acceptance does not connect the shipped handler to completion evidence, receipt recovery or release enforcement.
- **Decision:** Handle the reserved `gregale_queue_canary: v1` payload before business jobs. Require its exact three-field shape, UUID, binding and valid deployment capability list. A recognized canary completes a safe no-op and returns success; malformed or undeclared probes fail without reaching business handling. Keep ordinary job behavior. Deployment capability and publication consent remain explicit, separate API operations. Capability is customer intent, not dispatch authority or proof of business effects.
- **Native journey:** Deploy exact embedded source through the SDK's ordinary multipart uploader and reconcile its manifest binding through the public queue API. Require parked-guest restore, first-attempt exact-deployment completion, public release-policy enforcement, scheduler restart and durable receipt resumption after discarding an admission response. Count independent invocation rows to reject replacement/duplicate publication. Test cancelled/expired evidence, rejected replay of a completed canary, and a second zero-traffic deployment that cannot use the first deployment's completion. Only fresh completion on the second deployment authorizes its promotion. No scheduler receipts, guest completion or promotion evidence are seeded.
- **Isolation:** Use a migrated disposable catalog and ordinary daemon harness. Complete binding inventory also needs the PostgreSQL catalog service: configure a disabled unused backend with a fixture-only placeholder credential, no database rows, provisioning, usage or health collection. No provider resources or real credentials are needed. A designated idle host, shared acceptance lock and before/after leak checks remain mandatory.
- **Evidence:** Version-1 queue evidence requires all nine ordered phases, parent/package passes and one bounded fragmented cleanup identity after registered cleanup. Bind the clean revision and actual executable/guest/kernel/builder bundle to exact source/build provenance, both deployment layers and the function's immutable published Node base. Check its base and guest-init hashes against the runtime release and retain the same source/base across both deployments. Rehash after daemon shutdown before temporary files are removed. Private no-overwrite report publication requires cleanup, clean source and explicit host class; nested diagnostics cannot qualify native execution. Reports expire after 24 hours.
- **Limits:** Completion proves safe handler handling and durable acknowledgement, not application-specific effects or exactly-once business execution. The native replay phase rejects an attempt to replay already-completed work; existing portable PostgreSQL authority tests separately cover invalidation after actual replay-generation changes. Portable checks and tagged compilation do not establish native VM or host leak qualification. No production API, schema, quota or provisioning policy changes.
